Questions teams ask first

How do I start, and when do I pay?

Sign up for free, import a repo and choose what to audit. You see the exact price before anything is charged, and the audit starts when you approve it. The $50 credit for new accounts covers up to half of an order and expires after six months.

Guide: run your first audit

Does QuillShield replace a manual audit?

No. QuillShield is an AI smart contract audit that runs between manual audits. It reviews every change, and you can request a manual audit from QuillAudits.

Which languages does QuillShield support?

Solidity is supported today, for Foundry projects. More languages are coming, one at a time.

How long does a QuillShield audit take?

The demo vault at the top of this page took 2h 7m, and the researcher’s lending contract took 2h 30m. Larger codebases take longer. Findings appear while the audit runs, so your team can start fixing before it finishes.

How are findings checked before they reach me?

A separate reviewer agent tries to disprove every finding before you see it. Each one that holds up arrives with the exact lines, the impact, a recommended fix and, on full audits, the proof of concept result. If you want a human check as well, you can ask a QuillAudits auditor to verify specific findings from the audit page.

How expert review works

Can a failing check stop a merge?

Yes, on GitHub, where your plan includes branch protection or rulesets. Make the QuillShield check required and a pull request cannot merge while it has a High or Critical finding, or one at the threshold you set. Each full audit comes with five pull request checks, and more can be added. They need a GitHub or GitLab.com repo, so an uploaded zip gets a full audit and no checks.

QuillShield is building the security layer between AI and money, so you can keep shipping between audits.
